# Open-Source Adoption Gate

No candidate in this repository is pre-cleared for legal use. Before adding a dependency or copying code/assets, record:

1. Project URL, exact version/tag/commit, retrieval date, and provenance.
2. SPDX license identifier and full license text at that exact revision.
3. All direct and transitive dependencies, plugins, buildpacks, base images, and bundled assets.
4. Copyright and attribution/NOTICE obligations.
5. Patent grant, patent-termination clauses, contribution terms, and any non-license restrictions.
6. Trademark and branding requirements (a software license does not automatically grant trademark rights).
7. Security advisories, maintenance status, release integrity, and SBOM.
8. Whether modifications or distribution trigger source-disclosure or other obligations.
9. A replacement plan behind ModuCraft-owned interfaces.
10. Reviewer, decision, date, and evidence links.

A permissive license is not a patent-clearance opinion and cannot guarantee freedom to operate. Escalate material patent or licensing uncertainty to qualified counsel.
